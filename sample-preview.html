UI/UX Designer & Accessibility (WCAG 2.1) Evaluation

Professional assessment of the requested color combination for high-end luxury fashion e-commerce:

✓ Background (#f7f5ef) — Excellent
The warm alabaster/linen background brings an editorial, warm luxury feel reminiscent of European fashion houses (Celine, The Row, Jacquemus). Much softer on the eyes than harsh pure white (#ffffff).
! Sage (#9caf88) as Text — Readability Watchout
Contrast of #9caf88 text on #f7f5ef background is only 2.16:1 (fails WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum). For optimal UX, #9caf88 should be used for borders, tags, decorative lines, and architectural surfaces, while text uses deep forest ink (#162217) or deep sage (#384e36).
✓ Buttons (#e8412c) — High Affordance
The poppy vermilion creates an immediate focal point that draws user eyes to key actions ("Shop Collection", "Inquire", "Book Fitting"). It gives warmth and vitality to the soft organic background.
